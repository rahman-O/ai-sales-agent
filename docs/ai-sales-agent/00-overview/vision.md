# Vision

Build a tenant-isolated sales assistant that turns a customer conversation into a useful answer, qualified lead, or confirmed appointment, with a human able to intervene at any time. The first vertical can be clinic reception; the domain uses customers, services, staff, and bookings rather than patients, diagnoses, or treatment records.

Success means reliable business actions and accountable operations, not human impersonation. Disclose automation in the tenant's approved greeting. The assistant recommends actions; authenticated backend commands validate and execute them.

Start with one business location per tenant and WhatsApp text conversations. Keep channel transport separate from conversation and agent logic. Add another vertical through service data, approved knowledge, and qualification configuration before considering new infrastructure.

The north-star outcome is verified attended bookings attributed to assisted leads, with cost and human effort visible. Bookings alone are an early proxy, not revenue. See [scope](product-scope.md) and [outcomes](../11-product-metrics/business-outcomes.md).
