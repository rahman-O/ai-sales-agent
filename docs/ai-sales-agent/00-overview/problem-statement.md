# Problem statement

Businesses lose inquiries when staff cannot respond promptly, answers vary, customer details are scattered, and follow-ups are forgotten. A generic chatbot can answer prose but cannot safely reserve a staff member, verify a price, or preserve ownership when a receptionist intervenes.

The platform must join durable conversations with structured business commands. A reply is not proof of a booking: only a committed booking result is. A successful model response is not a delivered message: channel acknowledgements and receipts must be tracked separately.

Discovery must interview an owner and at least two operators from the selected pilot business; measure inquiry volume, response delay, current booking source, missed leads, and duplicate appointment rate. Record a baseline rather than inventing improvement claims. Validate Arabic dialect needs using consented, de-identified examples.

Primary failure to avoid: automation looks responsive while silently losing messages or making promises staff cannot honor. Compare pilot outcomes against the measured baseline and review failed conversations weekly. [Discovery](../14-roadmap/phase-00-discovery.md) owns this evidence.
