# Phase 00 product boundary review

Status: PROPOSED; human product approval required.

## Provisional vertical

The first vertical is a dental clinic. The product is a reception and sales/booking assistant, not a clinical system. It can describe approved services, quote authoritative catalog prices, gather minimal contact and service interest, propose availability, create/cancel/reschedule confirmed appointments, and transfer to reception staff. It must not diagnose, triage, recommend treatment, collect medical history, prescribe, interpret symptoms, or represent emergency advice. Apparent clinical or urgent requests use approved safe wording and human/emergency routing chosen by the clinic and jurisdiction.

## MVP included

One organization, one location, one WhatsApp number, text conversations, customers, durable conversations/messages, approved organization knowledge, service catalog and prices, staff and working hours, leads, internal booking subject to Q02, human takeover/resume, agent/tool/audit traces, and basic usage/cost visibility. Multiple staff members are compatible with the one-location boundary; one staff resource is allocated per booking.

The MVP proves the complete inquiry-to-human-control path. It does not mean public launch: provider sandbox evidence, operational UI, security tests, recovery behavior, and human coverage remain gates.

## Explicitly excluded

Instagram, voice, payments/deposits, mobile applications, external CRM, complex campaigns, multi-location, external-calendar synchronization, group/recurring/multi-resource bookings, clinical records, custom model fine-tuning, microservices, Kubernetes, and unrestricted automation.

## Pilot boundary

After the one-organization MVP passes, a controlled pilot may expand to no more than five approved dental organizations, one location each, text only, with consent-aware reminders, basic funnel/cost/outcome metrics, measured security/load/recovery evidence, trained operators, support coverage, stop conditions, and kill switches. This ceiling remains proposed and does not promise simultaneous rollout.

## Validation needed

Interview one clinic owner and at least two operators. Capture current inquiry channels, service/price ownership, appointment system, service durations/buffers, staff schedules/overrides, cancellation/reschedule policies, coverage hours, sensitive information customers commonly volunteer, volume baseline, and failure cases. Walk through price inquiry, vague Iraqi Arabic time, booking conflict, cancellation, human request, and off-hours escalation. Product acceptance requires named clinic/country, explicit authority decisions, and approval of included/excluded scope.
