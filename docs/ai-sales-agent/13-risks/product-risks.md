# Product risks

Validate business process ownership, staffing, consent and outcome definitions before writing feature code.

- [R05: Existing calendar remains authoritative](risk-register.md) — Resolve Q02; bring synchronization into scope if required. Closure gate: P00/P07.
- [R08: Sensitive clinic data collected without approved policy](risk-register.md) — Minimize data, prohibit clinical advice, resolve region/retention/processing terms. Closure gate: P00/P14.
- [R12: No staff coverage for escalations](risk-register.md) — Named coverage rota, queue-age alert and approved off-hours message. Closure gate: P09/P14.
- [R14: Misleading funnel or revenue claims](risk-register.md) — Evidence-linked outcomes, cohort definitions and replay-safe projections. Closure gate: P12.

The register owns severity, accountability and release-blocking triggers. Record realized incidents and mitigation evidence there; do not duplicate divergent risk scores across documents.
