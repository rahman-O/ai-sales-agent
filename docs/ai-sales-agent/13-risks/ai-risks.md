# AI risks

Quality averages cannot waive hard safety and authorization gates.

- [R03: False success or invented price](risk-register.md) — Structured tools and backend-rendered action confirmation. Closure gate: P04/P07/P14.
- [R07: Prompt injection or poisoned knowledge](risk-register.md) — Limited tools, approval pipeline, provenance and adversarial tests. Closure gate: P04/P05.
- [R11: Iraqi Arabic or timezone misunderstanding](risk-register.md) — Native-speaker evaluation and explicit localized confirmation. Closure gate: P04/P07/P14.

The register owns severity, accountability and release-blocking triggers. Record realized incidents and mitigation evidence there; do not duplicate divergent risk scores across documents.
