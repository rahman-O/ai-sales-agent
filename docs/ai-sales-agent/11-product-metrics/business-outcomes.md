# Business outcomes

BookingCreated proves reservation, AttendanceRecorded proves operator-observed attendance, and RevenueRecorded proves an independently verified monetary amount. Keep these distinct. A service price snapshot is booked value, not collected revenue; a WON lead is attended opportunity, not financial settlement.

Pilot attendance is entered by staff with actor/time/source and corrected through audited history. Revenue may be manually verified with a business reference, or remain unavailable. Do not require a payment gateway or fabricated revenue values for MVP. Refunds/corrections use signed records linked to the original outcome.

Review weekly: unanswered inquiries, time to first useful response, qualified leads, booking conflicts, attendance evidence completeness, human workload, false claims and AI cost. Proposed pilot success requires no unresolved critical integrity/safety defects and product-approved improvement against baseline; exact conversion uplift targets are a P00 decision.

Analytics should reveal where evidence is missing. Unknown attendance/revenue cannot be silently interpreted as absence or failure. [Funnel](funnel.md) supplies denominator rules.
